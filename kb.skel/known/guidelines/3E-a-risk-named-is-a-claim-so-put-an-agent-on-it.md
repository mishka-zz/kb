title: A risk named is a claim like any other, so put an agent on it rather than voicing it
kind: fact
noted: 2026-09-12
tags: method, review, agents, carried
provenance: judged: worked out on another record, where a naming decision was
    closed with one voiced risk, and the owner showed in a sentence that the risk
    was not small but empty - the thing the name would supposedly outlive could
    not be replaced without rewriting what carried the name
related: 2B, 2F, 1B, 1R, 2E
---

**The words are the trigger.** Risk, danger, this could break, we would have to
rewrite, stop and reconsider. Each of those is a prediction about what would
happen, and a prediction is a claim - held to what any claim is held to.
Voicing one costs the reader a decision and costs the claim nothing.

**The shape of the occasion.** A package was to be named for the library it
wraps, and the naming was closed with *the one risk is that if the library is
ever replaced the name becomes a lie*. The package is an interface to that
library and is built on its API, so replacing the library means rewriting the
package whatever it is called. The risk took no measurement to dissolve - only
somebody looking at it who was not the one who wrote it.

**What to do instead.** Put an agent on it, at the level the risk is stated at,
before the sentence is written rather than after. [[2B]] already says an agent
to argue a design against belongs *before* it is written; this is the trigger
that sends one, and it is cheaper than the two [[2B]] names because the question
is one paragraph.

**Why an agent rather than more thought.** The hedge and the design come from
the same head, so the head that produced the hedge is the one least able to see
it is empty - the same asymmetry [[1B]] uses for a reviewer's finding and
[[1R]] for an entry's stated cause. What is wanted is a reader who did not
write it.

**The failure this does not fix.** An agent that agrees is worth nothing here,
for the reason [[2F]] gives about the consulting expert: agreement repeated back
reads as confirmation and is not. Brief it to argue against, and read only what
it disagrees with. And where the risk survives the agent, [[2E]] applies - take
the decision rather than leaving the hedge standing for somebody else.
