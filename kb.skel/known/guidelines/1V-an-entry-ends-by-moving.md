Editing the old entry in place is the one move that has no home, because it
destroys the record the pair exists to preserve: what was believed, what
replaced it, and why. The full procedure is `SKILL.md` beside `kb.py` under *Ending it some
other way*; this line exists so that a session that has not read that section
this week still knows there is one.

The endings, each a `mv` plus the fields the destination wants:

- A **superseded decision** goes to `moot/`, with `because` and `closed`, and
  `related` naming what replaced it. The new decision is written first, so the
  old one has something to point at.
- A **fact that turned out false** leaves `known/` for `refused/`, with
  `because` saying what was believed, what is true, and how the error survived.
  Never left in place with a doubting note - a wrong measurement stated calmly
  is worse than none.
- A fact **overtaken by our own code changing** is not false and is not history:
  a subject describing our code describes it as it is, so the entry is
  corrected to the present tense and its provenance re-pointed.
