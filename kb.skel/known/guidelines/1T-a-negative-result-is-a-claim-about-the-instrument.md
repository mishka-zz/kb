**A proxy inside a check has the opposite obligation to one inside a demand.** A
demand may be generous, because generous costs resources; a check may not,
because generous costs the complaint. A check built on a quantity that leans the
wrong way does not fail loudly - it returns clean, and clean is the answer
everyone was hoping for.

**So a sweep that finds nothing has measured the instrument.** Before believing
a negative result, exhibit the positive one: construct a case the check *must*
fire on, and watch it fire. Sixty clean runs and one demonstrated firing are
different pieces of evidence, and only the second says the sweep could have
spoken.

**Say which way it errs, next to it.** A bias chosen and written down beside the
proxy is a stated limit. The same bias unnoticed reads as handled, which is
worse than no check, because it also spends the attention that would have gone
to writing a real one.

**A third shape, and it is an A/B digest.** A change moved some fields onto a
type of their own, and a digest of the two trees over the whole corpus agreed
on every line. What the change could most plausibly have moved was the order of
a list the digest did not hold, and an adversarial tester rewriting that list as
two loops moved hundreds of rows and not one digest line - so the agreement had
never been evidence about order. The positive case for a digest is a **mutant of the
tree** rather than an input: break the code the digest is meant to be watching
and check that the digest moves. Doing that first also says whether the digest
is blunt, which is the other thing a null comparison cannot tell you about
itself. [[2W]] is the neighbouring fault, where the comparison itself was built
blind.
