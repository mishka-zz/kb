As first written down, the entry was a disagreement between two **estimators** -
one taken over the whole population, one over a narrower slice, with the second
assumed to be the truth because it was the narrower question.

It was the worse of the two. Scored against something outside both, the
estimator the entry proposed is out by more than that reference is uncertain by.
So the entry had a fault of the right shape pointing the wrong way, and every
step of its argument was internally consistent, because nothing in it left the
pair.

Two answers disagreeing establishes only that they disagree. Before an entry
claims one of them is wrong, something outside both has to say so: a closed
form, a conservation law, a symmetry, a hand count over a set small enough to
do by hand. That is available more often than it looks.

## The same thing when they agree

A round-trip test asserted that a thing and its copy through a file get one
answer from a layer, by holding the two answers to each other and to nothing
else, under a tolerance tight enough that agreement looked like proof.

It is the same failure in the mirror. A comparison sees only what one side has
and the other does not, so a fault both sides carry passes it - and both sides
were the same code reading the same input, which is the case where they most
reliably agree while being wrong. When the tolerance had to be loosened, nothing
else in the file was watching.

The repair was not a better tolerance. It was asking the criteria of **each side
separately**, which costs nothing where both records already exist, and it is
what a comparison cannot be made to do by tightening it.

So the rule reads both ways. Two answers disagreeing establishes only that they
disagree; two answers agreeing establishes only that they agree. What settles
either is a criterion from outside the pair - and where the pair is two runs of
one layer, "outside" means a property of the thing itself, not a third run.
