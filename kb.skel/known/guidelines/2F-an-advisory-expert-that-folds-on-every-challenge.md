A consulted expert produces clean structure and occasionally an angle nobody
here had. It also concedes every point it is pushed on, including the ones it
should keep, so its **agreement is not evidence** - it is what it does whatever
the input.

Its **disagreement is worth reading**, and it is worth most on self-contained
algebra, where the answer is checkable without the repository. On a claim about
this codebase, or an open question with no closed form under it, it agrees with
whoever spoke last.

It is opt-in and advisory: consult it when asked to, never on the way past.
Headless, it auto-denies anything needing to run a command and returns empty
with a warning, so paste what it must read into the prompt.
