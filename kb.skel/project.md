What this record is about. The fields above are the tool's and `schema.md`
beside `kb.py` says what each means; every one is optional and comma-separated,
and the record's own directory is always owned and always a citer. Fill them in
the day the record is copied into a project, and put here, below the fences,
whatever a reader of the record should know about the project that its own
instruction does not say - which trees are released on their own cadence, what
the bars are measured in, which subject holds behaviour we do not control.

The guidelines under `known/guidelines/` came with the copy. Each is tagged
`carried`: the mechanism is whole and the case is somebody else's, and the first
time a case turns up here the entry is rewritten on it and the tag comes off.
